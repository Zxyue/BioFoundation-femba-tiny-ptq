ptq_activation_observer.py adapts the unfused full-window Mamba inference equations
by adding explicit fake-quant boundaries, retaining the existing parameters,
using torch CUDA convolution, and calling the original CUDA selective_scan_fn.
It does not use the ARES QuantSSM recurrence or substitute its projection rank.
